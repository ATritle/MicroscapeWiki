# World map artwork

`microscape-world-map.png` is the original, unmodified Microscape world-map image
from https://microscape.cc/play/assets/microscape-world-map-8vzsaSaK.png.
Retrieved November 21, 2026; native dimensions are 2000 × 1800.
Artwork belongs to Microscape and its credited creators:
https://microscape.cc/credits/.

Pin coordinates, activities, NPCs, shop stock and drops come from the public game
client `index-BsSHHy-E.js`. Its source URL, checked date and SHA-256 are recorded
in `docs/world-map-data.js`. Interior chambers are explicitly associated with
their documented world-map entrance, not assigned an invented overworld position.
